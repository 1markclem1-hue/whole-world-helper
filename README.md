Whole World Helper

A calming, visual, music-supported web app for students with Autism and Down Syndrome

🌍 Overview

Whole World Helper is an evidence-based, accessibility-focused web app designed to support students with Autism and Down Syndrome, especially those who benefit from:

Visual schedules

Predictable routines

AAC-style communication support

Emotion check-ins

Music-based self-regulation

The app is built around the students’ favorite song:
🎵 “He’s Got the Whole World in His Hands”
which plays directly in the app to support regulation, transitions, and positive reinforcement.

Whole World Helper can be used on any school computer, Chromebook, or classroom device through a simple web browser. No downloads or installation needed.

💡 Backed by Evidence-Based Practices

Whole World Helper is intentionally built on research-supported strategies for learners with Autism and Down Syndrome:

✔️ Visual Schedules / First–Then Boards

Research consistently shows that First–Then supports reduce anxiety, improve transitions, and increase independence for autistic learners.

✔️ Choice Boards & Simplified AAC

Providing structured choices increases engagement and reduces frustration for both non-speaking autistic students and students with Down Syndrome.

✔️ Emotion Identification with Icons

Visual emotion supports help students communicate needs and self-advocate even when expressive language is limited.

✔️ Music-Integrated Regulation

Songs students love—especially predictable, repetitive songs—can lower stress and improve cooperation.
For many students, “He’s Got the Whole World in His Hands” is calming and grounding.

✔️ Consistent, Low-Cognitive-Load UI

Students with processing delays benefit from one-step-at-a-time screens, predictable layouts, and minimal text.

🎵 Key Features
1. Built-in Song Player

A calm, clean interface that plays “He’s Got the Whole World in His Hands” with one click.

2. Two Custom Profiles

Each student has a tailored experience:

Student A – Autism (nonverbal): Clear visual choices, sensory break options

Student B – Down Syndrome: Music-rich options, art/story choices, simple language

3. First–Then Visual Board

A highly customizable First–Then system that lets teachers set:

First: Work / Task / Routine

Then: Song Break / Sensory Break / Snack / All Done

4. Emotion Check-In

Students tap feelings like: Happy, OK, Sad, Mad, Tired
Teacher instantly sees their emotional state.

5. Choice Board Communication

Students can tap:

“I want song”

“I want break”

“I want water”

“I want snack”

(and many others depending on their profile)

The choice appears as a clear, readable message for staff.

🧩 Who This App Is For

Whole World Helper works especially well for:

Non-speaking autistic students

Students with Down Syndrome

Students who rely on routine and predictability

Students who use AAC or picture-based supports

Early childhood, elementary, and special education classrooms

🚀 How to Use

1. Open the app in any browser.
2. Select the student profile at the top.
3. Press “Play Song” to begin the familiar calming routine.
4. Use the First–Then board, choice board, and emotion check-in during class.

This tool is meant to assist teachers, paraprofessionals, and therapists in creating a calm, structured, supportive learning environment.
